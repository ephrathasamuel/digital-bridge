# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want taking part in this project to be a welcoming experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible
or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience,
education, socio economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, language, or sexual identity and
orientation. This project exists for disabled learners, so their voices come first.

## Expected behaviour

* Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new or who use assistive technology.
* Give and accept feedback about the work, not the person.
* Write plainly. Not everyone reads English as a first language.
* Respect people's privacy. Never share someone's disability or personal details without their permission.

## Unacceptable behaviour

* Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes or language.
* Mocking someone's disability, language or accent.
* Publishing other people's private information.
* Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Report problems privately to the maintainer through the repository's **Security** tab (Report a vulnerability) or by
asking for a private channel in a short public issue that contains no details. Reports are handled confidentially.
Maintainers may remove comments, commits or issues, and may ban contributors who break this code.
