Features

Everything a hard form is missing

Built with disabled learners in Addis Ababa. Every part works by keyboard, screen reader, voice and touch.

  • Answer by voice

    Speak any answer. You see what was heard and can change it. Nothing is sent without your OK.

  • Hear every question

    Each field's name, hint and current answer read aloud, in any language your device or our voices know.

  • ሀ

    Type Amharic easily

    Type selam and get ሰላም. No Amharic keyboard needed.

  • Colours for every eye

    A palette safe for all types of colour blindness, plus high contrast, dark and bigger text.

  • Private by design

    Answers stay on the page. Support needs are never shared with employers unless you choose to.

  • Made for slow internet

    Small downloads, answers saved on the device, and no time limits. Nothing is lost when the signal drops.

How it works

Three steps, on any form

  1. Open the help bar

    Press Accessibility help at the bottom of the page, or Alt + Shift + H.

  2. Pick a field

    Click or Tab to any box: name, phone, a long answer. The bar knows which one you mean.

  3. Listen, speak or type

    Hear the question, answer by voice or in Amharic, check it, and carry on yourself.

Use it anywhere

From no code to a full integration

Use the extension on sites you do not own, or add Digital Bridge to your own site in one line.

  • No code

    Browser extension

    Works on LinkedIn, job boards and government forms, in Chrome and Edge.

    1. Download the code from GitHub.
    2. Open chrome://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
    3. Choose Load unpacked, then the apps/extension folder.
    Get the extension
  • One line

    Help bar on your site

    Adds the same help bar to every form on your pages.

    <script src="https://digital-bridge-six.vercel.app/db-assist.js" defer></script>
  • Full

    Widgets, API and LMS

    Ready made sign up, jobs and Ask widgets, a REST API, and LTI 1.3 for Moodle and Canvas.

    <db-signup lang="am"></db-signup>

Voices

A voice for every language

Read aloud tries each source in order until one speaks your language. Keys stay on your server, never in the browser.

  1. First

    Device voices

    Free and offline, from the phone or computer.

  2. Then

    Open source voices

    Meta's MMS voices: more than 1,100 languages, Amharic included, through your API.

  3. Or

    Your own service

    Connect any text to speech service with one URL, such as a self hosted Piper server.
