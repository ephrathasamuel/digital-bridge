Teach your own signs

Show a sign to the camera a few times and give it a word. Then sign, and the words appear as text you can read aloud or copy.

Your camera pictures never leave this device. A hand tracker (Google MediaPipe, Apache 2.0) runs in your browser and downloads once when you turn the camera on. The signs you teach are saved only in this browser. Use Save to a file to share them with your community. This early version knows still hand shapes, not signs with movement, and it only knows the signs people teach it.
