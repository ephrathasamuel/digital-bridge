Privacy: Digital Bridge help bar

This covers the Digital Bridge browser extension, the bookmark button and the db-assist.js script. Last updated 23 November 2026.

What stays on your device

  • Your answers in web forms. The help bar never sends, saves or submits them.
  • Your help bar settings (language, display options), kept in the browser storage of each website.
  • Speech recognition runs in your browser. Depending on the browser, its maker may process the audio (for example Google for Chrome). Digital Bridge receives no audio.

What may be sent

  • Only when you press Read aloud (or turn on Read as I move) and your device has no voice for your language: the words being read, and the language code, go to the Digital Bridge voice service to make audio. They are not stored or logged.
  • Nothing else. No tracking, no analytics, no advertising, no account.

Permissions

The extension asks for no special permissions. It runs on the pages you visit so it can show the help bar there.

Contact

Questions or problems: open an issue on GitHub, or report privately through the repository's Security tab.
