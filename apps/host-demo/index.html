Forms everyone can fill in.

Digital Bridge adds voice answers, read aloud, Amharic and English, sign language help and colour modes to sign up and job forms, so disabled learners and job seekers can finish them without help. Use it as a browser extension on any site, or add one line of code to your own.

What it does

Built with disabled learners in Addis Ababa. Every feature works by keyboard, screen reader and touch.

  • Answer by voice

    Speak any answer, check what was heard, and change it before it is used. Nothing is sent without your OK.

  • Read aloud, any language

    Questions and answers are read aloud with the device's voices, open source voices or a voice service you choose.

  • Sign language help

    Picture cards for common questions and a signed video you can keep on your device for an interpreter.

  • Colours for every eye

    A default palette that works for all types of colour blindness, plus high contrast and dark. States never rely on colour alone.

  • Private by design

    Support needs stay on your device and are never shared with employers unless you choose to share them.

  • Works on slow internet

    Answers are saved on the device and sent when the connection returns. No time limits, nothing is lost.

Use it anywhere

Three ways in, from no code at all to a full integration.

  • No code

    Browser extension

    Adds a help bar to any form on any website, for example LinkedIn, a job board or a government form: voice answers, read aloud, Amharic typing and colour modes.

    1. Download the code from GitHub.
    2. Open chrome://extensions or edge://extensions and turn on Developer mode.
    3. Choose Load unpacked and pick the apps/extension folder.
    Get the extension
  • One line

    Help bar on your own site

    Site owners add the same help bar to every form on their pages with one script tag.

    <script src="https://digital-bridge-six.vercel.app/db-assist.js" defer></script>
  • Full

    Widgets, API and LMS

    Ready made sign up, jobs and Ask widgets, a REST API, and LTI 1.3 for Moodle and Canvas.

    <db-signup lang="am"></db-signup>
    Read the install guide

Voices for any language

Choose where the read aloud voice comes from. Keys stay on your server, never in the browser.

  • Device voices

    Free and offline. Uses the voices already on the phone or computer.

  • Open source voices

    Meta's MMS voices cover more than 1,100 languages, including Amharic, through your Digital Bridge API.

  • Your own voice service

    Connect any text to speech service with a URL, for example a self hosted Piper server or a paid provider.
